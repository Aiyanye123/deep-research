# Reading Supplied Materials

Choose tools supported by the host and check extraction against the source where
an error could change the conclusion. Keep page, sheet, cell, or image locations
for consequential evidence; report unreadable or unavailable content.

- **PDF:** Check reading order in multi-column pages, footnotes, tables, and text
  split across pages. Compare important passages and numbers with rendered pages;
  use OCR for scans and inspect uncertain characters in the image.
- **Spreadsheets:** Identify the relevant sheets, including hidden sheets when
  accessible, merged cells, headers, units, and formulas versus displayed values.
  Check whether filters, hidden rows, or stale formula results affect the analysis;
  do not infer contents that the available tool cannot expose.
- **Images and scans:** Inspect the full image, including crops of long or
  multi-page material, and verify OCR of names, numbers, punctuation, and table
  alignment against the image. Preserve page or image locations for citations.
- **Documents and slides:** Check tables, notes, captions, and reading order when
  relevant; text extraction alone may omit layout-dependent relationships.

Use only checks relevant to the task and material. When a meaningful ambiguity
remains, qualify the finding or request an accessible copy.
