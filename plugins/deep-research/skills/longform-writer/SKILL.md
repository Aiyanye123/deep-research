# Longform Writer

Develop the requested work with enough room for the question, material, and voice.
Once the research direction is confirmed, drafting can uncover new questions and
send the work back to sources or interpretation. An outline is useful when it
helps; neither a completed evidence ledger nor another skill's approval is a
prerequisite to writing.

Always carry out the article-writing routine in `deep-research`: establish focus
and organization, draft through the material, review and revise the completed
structure, then polish language and check affected meaning. These actions are
required even when the plugin's other skills are selected as needed. Read
`prose-humanizer` before drafting and use it again for the final pass; do not
reserve all of its guidance for sentence cleanup after the article is written.
Keep manuscript drafting with the lead; do not default to parallel chapter
writing from research summaries. In the complete workflow, obtain a subagent's
whole-manuscript structural review using `insight-architect`. The lead decides
and implements changes and performs final language polishing using
`prose-humanizer`. Follow the same authorship boundaries in orchestration mode.

## Shape The Work

Choose the structure for the genre and reader. A report may need visible
conditions and conclusions; technical work needs stable terms and operating
details; an essay may move through scenes, details, tensions, or delayed
explanations. Literary and cultural criticism can develop a reading, complicate
it, or leave a meaningful uncertainty without making every section prove a new
thesis.

Use the requested length and format as constraints, not a reason to pad the work.
Draft by section or chapter when that supports sustained attention or installment
delivery. There is no word-count threshold that automatically requires a special
workflow. Write finished prose, keeping research scaffolding and agent process
out of the reader's experience.

## Write And Research Together

Use evidence naturally and keep citations in the requested format. Check important
facts, numbers, quotations, disputed claims, and key conclusions as needed.
Separate established facts from proposed explanations or hypotheses where the
distinction matters, while allowing original synthesis and exploration. When a
new idea needs evidence, investigate it rather than forcing it into the existing
outline. If material conflicts or leaves a consequential gap, make that visible
and adjust the conclusion.

Let examples and details do useful work. Stay with important material long enough
to show how a change, arrangement, action, or condition produces the effect being
discussed. Give the reader something to examine alongside the judgment; a concrete
detail followed by generic praise or condemnation is not yet an explanation.
Develop selected material according to its importance, without turning every
example into the same miniature argument or adding unverified scene details.
Avoid source dumping, repetitive claim/evidence summaries, and sections that each
sound like a fresh standalone answer. A figure should sit near the passage it
clarifies; preserve its units,
source notes, caption, and any needed cross-reference, and explain the takeaway
without restating every value.

Develop reasoning across connected paragraphs. Do not give every paragraph the
same setup, concession, contrast, and closing verdict, or restate the central
thesis whenever a new example appears. Let an observation lead to its explanation
and consequences without repeatedly stopping for an aphorism. Keep related
material together and use paragraph breaks or headings where the thought needs
them, without a prescribed paragraph length or ending pattern.

For criticism and essays, sustain a viewpoint attentive to the experience of the
subject. Use supported particulars and direct judgment rather than generalized
labels for depth or emotion. Actual distinctions and qualifications belong where
they matter; do not manufacture an opposing position merely to sound incisive.
Formal and technical writing retain the organization their reader needs.

State the supported judgment and develop its reasons. Avoid cushioning each point
with preemptive rebuttals or blanket disclaimers; locate necessary limits beside
the claims they affect. Keep real uncertainty and competing explanations without
making the article a running defense against hypothetical criticism. Use concepts
when they clarify the material, explaining their meaning and actual relevance.
When a label adds no understanding, describe the action, relationship, conflict,
or mechanism directly. Keep precise terminology where the subject requires it.

## Maintain Continuity

Read relevant preceding sections before continuing. Keep names, terminology,
voice, chronology, and the developing argument coherent. Watch for repeated
examples, unexplained shifts, abandoned questions, and contradictions. If a later
discovery changes the central reading or conclusion, revise affected earlier
passages.

Use a brief continuity note only when length, complexity, or a handoff makes it
helpful. Record what the next writing step actually needs, such as unresolved
questions, established terms, or a passage needing revision. Do not require a
template, a note after every chapter, or separate style and visual manifests.

## Finish The Deliverable

After completing the manuscript, read and use `prose-humanizer` for the required
final reading and polishing pass. First obtain the structural review under the
division of labor and implement useful revisions; then the lead reads and
polishes the resulting expression, rhythm, transitions, repetition, continuity,
and fit to the work. Perform both actions even when the draft appears fluent.
Do not require the lead to repeat the delegated structural audit as a separate
stage; its full-text language pass and responsibility for revisions remain.
No separate drafts or records are needed.
Use focused factual checking for material risks and factual changes made during
editing, protecting quotations, qualifications, and the meaning of conclusions.
Deliver the complete requested text or agreed installment; do not attach management
records unless the user needs them. Older session-management tools are available
only when explicitly chosen, not a default drafting requirement.
