# Prose Humanizer

Find a voice suited to the material and reader, and edit prose that feels
mechanical, inflated, or indistinct. Use this skill while drafting or when a
passage needs revision, and always perform a final reading and polishing pass
before delivering a completed manuscript. Read and apply this guidance before
drafting or rewriting as part of the required article-writing routine. Natural
drafting does not replace the final pass. Flexible skill selection does not make
structure review or expression polishing optional. Neither requires a style
sheet, a completed audit, or two versions of the manuscript.

Final language polishing belongs to the lead agent, including in orchestration
mode. Structural review may be delegated, and is assigned to a subagent in the
complete workflow using `insight-architect` and the complete current manuscript.
The reviewer returns located problems and revision proposals; the lead decides
and implements changes, then reads and polishes the resulting text itself. Do not
substitute a reviewer summary or several separately polished sections for this
whole-text language pass. Follow `deep-research`'s division of labor and any
explicit user restrictions; no second structural-audit stage is required.

## Let The Material Set The Voice

Consider the genre, subject, reader relationship, and user's examples. When a
sample is available, observe sentence rhythm, paragraph movement, diction,
punctuation, opinion, and useful irregularities without copying its content.
Infer what makes the sample work across passages: how it selects details, holds
a viewpoint, develops reasons, and lets a reader experience the subject. Do not
imitate its conspicuous phrases, metaphor density, or intensity of assertion.
Keep sample-specific content in the current task, not in reusable style rules.
When comparing an original and a preferred rewrite, distinguish improvements in
voice and movement from added facts, changed interpretations, and stronger claims.
Learn the useful editorial choices without treating every change as a stylistic
improvement or importing unsupported additions into the current manuscript.
Otherwise choose a fitting voice from the work itself. Publication platform
alone is not a style preset.
The subject alone does not determine the article type, tone, or degree of
criticism. Follow the user's purpose and preferred writing; genre guidance applies
only where it fits that work, without imposing a category or argumentative stance.

Literary criticism can be essayistic, attentive to details, and willing to sustain
ambiguity. A review needs concrete examples and visible judgment. Public
explanation should follow the reader's questions. Narrative nonfiction may use
scenes only where evidence supports their details. Formal analysis needs
proportion and explicit limits; technical work benefits from direct language and
stable terms. Do not force these genres into one common shape.

For Chinese prose, consult `<plugin-root>/references/chinese-prose.md` when its
genre guidance helps, and `<plugin-root>/references/humanizer-zh.md` when diagnosing
mechanical wording. Apply them in context, not as compulsory profiles or a list
of constructions to ban. The existing Chinese prose checker is optional when the
user chooses it or a specific prose issue warrants it; its warnings are prompts
for judgment, not automatic edits. English work does not need the Chinese tools.

## Edit Meaning And Movement

Improve the order of material, timing of explanations, sentence variety, and
paragraph movement. Find what carries each connected passage: an observation,
development, relationship, mechanism, or discovery. Bring that material into focus
and develop what it changes in the reader's understanding. When a passage only
announces an abstract conclusion, return to available material and rebuild its
explanation rather than adding emphatic wording. This is an editorial choice,
not a prescribed sequence for every paragraph.

Remove generic introductions, inflated significance, vague authority, promotional
language, repetitive scaffolding, chat residue, and jargon that hides who did what.
Do not replace them with another uniform prose pattern.

Preserve concrete details, defensible judgment, unresolved tension, and meaningful
irregularities. First person can be useful in an essay, but never invent personal
experience. Repetition can help formal or technical precision. Punctuation,
contrast, headings, and a measured rhythm are not defects merely because a
checker recognizes them.

Literary readings should retain close attention, multiple meanings, and a voice
that takes interpretive responsibility. They need not become neutral summaries or
hedge every sentence. Reports and technical documents need clear distinctions
and accurate conditions rather than ornamental narration.

## Chinese Prose

优先自然、具体、准确，让语言服务于内容。使用准确的动词、有效的细节和
有依据的判断，不用辞藻、抽象概念或宏大评价代替内容。用已有材料与来源中的
具体描述替代“很高级”“很有感觉”等空泛评价；缺少细节时不要捏造。

句子长短自然变化，长句应承担叙事、节奏或论证上的明确作用。保留思想的
复杂度和全文目标，不机械短句化、模板化。合适的文学修辞可以保留，但不能
顶替内容；这些原则不是固定禁词表。

最终润色应实际审阅套话、官腔、商业黑话、生造标签、不能增加信息的程度副词，
以及没有新增内容的总结和宏大拔高。必要时实质改写句子、段落与衔接，删去重复
空话，而不只是替换几个词。改写后确认论述层次、声音与必要信息仍然完整。

## Paragraphs And Whole-Text Rhythm

Edit connected passages as well as individual sentences. Repeated paragraph
patterns can make even fluent prose mechanical: a general judgment, an obligatory
qualification or reversal, then a quotable verdict. Do not close every paragraph
with a maxim, balanced contrast, rhetorical question, or restatement of its point.
Keep a strong ending where the argument earns it; elsewhere let detail, reasoning,
or the next question carry the passage onward. Do not replace this habit with a
rotation of prescribed ending types.

Keep related observation, source material, and interpretation together long enough
to complete the thought. Merge fragments or trim repeated verdicts when they
interrupt development. Separate a paragraph when the subject, emphasis, or pace
actually calls for it. Neither uniform short paragraphs nor uniformly long ones
provide natural rhythm. Use headings for useful divisions, not every local point.

Use contrast when the distinction is real and necessary. If consecutive passages
repeatedly correct an imagined misunderstanding, state the actual judgment and
explain its causes or evidence directly. Remove preemptive rebuttal chains and
blanket disclaimers that merely reassure an imagined critic. Put a necessary
qualification beside the claim it changes, explaining its actual effect on the
conclusion; do not repeat the same protection throughout the article. Preserve
substantive counterarguments, causal limits, and uncertainty. Directness does not
require stronger certainty or a harsher verdict. Swapping connectives leaves the
repeated reasoning pattern intact.

Give abstractions an intelligible referent: who acts, what changes, how a situation
is experienced, and why it matters. In an essay or criticism, allow an attentive
reader's frustration, hesitation, amusement, or mixed response to develop through
supported particulars and defensible interpretation. Do not describe emotions only
through abstract nouns or turn every response into a neutral definition. Formal
and technical work can achieve presence through precise explanation; they do not
need invented scenes, chatty asides, or decorative emotion.

Use theory to explain the material, not to supply an appearance of depth. When a
concept merely renames an observation, replace the label with the actual action,
relationship, conflict, or mechanism. When it adds explanatory power, make its
meaning and connection to the material intelligible to the intended reader.
Preserve definitions and exact terms required by academic or technical work;
neither jargon removal nor colloquial wording should erase a useful distinction.
Let emotional movement follow what the reader learns or notices, without forcing
a dramatic escalation or a predetermined moral verdict.

Introduce source material where it advances the thought. Integrate attribution
with the explanation rather than repeatedly interrupting it with a source summary
and a new mini-conclusion. Keep citations beside the claims they support; they
may move with those claims during restructuring. Do not remove source boundaries
or turn a limited inference into a certain fact for a smoother or sharper sentence.

Read across the paragraph endings and transitions during the final pass. When
they sound interchangeable or keep reannouncing the same thesis, revise the
connected passage, not just the final words. This is editorial attention, not a
counting rule, ban list, or separate audit. Keep the requested scope and substance.

## Preserve Factual Meaning

Keep quotations, citation links, names, dates, numbers, units, formulas, technical
terms, material conditions, and source boundaries accurate. Preserve the logic
and scope of necessary qualifications, not every original cautionary sentence.
Repeated hedges can be combined or rewritten, and purely defensive padding can
be removed without weakening the underlying claim's actual limits. Preserve
required structure, figure references, captions, and source notes. Do not paraphrase a
quotation or alter code and structured data to improve stylistic consistency.

Editing may change emphasis or ordering, but must not silently turn a hypothesis
into a fact, weaken a material caveat, or create unsupported detail. If an edit
changes factual meaning or exposes a consequential gap, check the affected source
and nearby reasoning, or use `evidence-auditor` for that focused question.
Research and editing can iterate naturally; a full new audit and an immutable
companion draft are unnecessary defaults.

Before returning a completed manuscript, perform two editorial actions in order:
obtain a whole-text structural review for focus, development, and continuity under
the division of labor, and have the lead revise connected passages where needed;
then have the lead read and polish the resulting expression, rhythm, transitions,
repetition, and fit to the work. Actually perform both actions while
preserving facts, quotations, necessary qualifications, and the meaning of the
conclusions. Do not skip either because the first draft sounds natural or because
skills are selected as needed. Review must preserve original thinking and a fitting
voice, including purposeful irregularities; it does not require a conventional
outline or a tidy resolution. Recheck affected meaning or facts after substantive
edits and polish passages changed by corrections. A limited passage edit needs
a contextual reading of that passage and its connections. No separate artifact
or completion report is required for either action.

Deliver the edited text without process narration or extra management files.
Older session-management tools remain optional only when the user explicitly
selects that workflow.
